Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains the data handled by the MIDI HUD web beta, how optional Google Drive synchronization works, and your data protection rights.

1. Who is responsible for processing

MIDI HUD is an independent software project operated from Portugal. The project operator determines the purposes and means of the processing described in this policy. For privacy questions and data rights requests, contact midihudapp@gmail.com.

The operator's full personal name or legal entity name has not yet been published and must be added before the identification requirement in GDPR Article 13 can be considered complete. You may request that identification using the contact address above.

2. Information processed

  • MIDI HUD library: repertoire lists, song and artist names, lead sheets, musical states, MIDI configuration, assignments, preferences and edits entered by the user.
  • Google account information, if connected: the Google account identifier, email address and temporary authorization to access the application's private Google Drive storage.
  • Local technical information: version identifiers, synchronization dates, local backups, change history and conflicting versions.
  • Voluntary correspondence: information sent when a user emails the contact address.

The application does not request your Google password or payment information. The currently published code does not include a proprietary behavioral analytics or advertising system.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Library information is processed to provide user-requested functions such as creating, editing, saving, displaying and synchronizing repertoires, under performance of the terms of use (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Connecting Google and syncing through Drive are optional and initiated with the user's express authorization, based on consent (Article 6(1)(a)). Limited technical records needed for security and recovery may be supported by legitimate interests in service integrity (Article 6(1)(f)).

Local features can be used without connecting a Google account, but cross-device synchronization will not be available.

4. Storage on your device and in Google Drive

The browser uses localStorage to hold the library, settings, synchronization state and backups. Short-lived OAuth authorization is stored temporarily in memory and in sessionStorage, not in a public database. The public OAuth client identifier may be stored locally.

When synchronization is enabled, MIDI HUD communicates directly with Google APIs and stores JSON library versions in the special appDataFolder within your own Google Drive account. This is application-specific storage, not general access to your personal Drive files. The requested access scope is drive.appdata plus basic account identification (openid and email).

When persistent authentication is enabled, MIDI HUD uses Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1 to manage trusted devices and store encrypted Google tokens, Google account identifiers, email addresses and session metadata. Your browser locally retains a revocable random device session token. Musical libraries continue to be stored on your device and in the private Google Drive application folder, not in a central Cloudflare repertoire database.

5. Data sharing and external services

Information required for authorization and synchronization is exchanged with Google, which hosts the application data in the user's Drive account. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical access data under its own practices. When enabled, persistent authentication additionally uses Cloudflare services to handle authenticated requests and retain encrypted credentials and device sessions. External services may process information outside the European Economic Area, subject to the legal safeguards and policies applicable to those providers.

MIDI HUD does not sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or disclose it to third parties for AI model training. Use of information received through Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Please also read Google's Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Retention and deletion

The local library remains in the browser until you delete it, clear site data or stop using that browser profile. Synced data and version history remain in the application's private Google Drive storage until deleted through relevant Google account controls or another available mechanism. The current version does not provide an integrated function for deleting the entire remote history.

Disconnecting Google or revoking authorization stops future access, but does not automatically delete local data, exported backups or versions already stored in Drive. Per-device authorizations, when available, are revocable and expire after an extended period without use. Authentication records and encrypted tokens stored in Cloudflare may require separate deletion upon request to the project contact. Emails to the contact address are retained for as long as needed to respond and meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Security

The app uses HTTPS and OAuth access limited to its own Drive storage. Access tokens are temporary; repertoire data is held locally and in the selected Google account. No system can guarantee absolute security. You should protect your devices and Google account, and periodically export a backup of your library.

8. Your data protection rights

Under the GDPR, where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Send requests to midihudapp@gmail.com. Google authorization can also be revoked from your Google Account security settings.

The project operator may have no direct access to a library held exclusively in the user's private Google Drive account; where this applies, guidance will be provided on using account tools. You may lodge a complaint with Portugal's supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The code currently published does not set first-party marketing cookies or include its own advertising or usage analytics service. It uses browser local storage for its functions and synchronization. Google authentication and hosting infrastructure may use cookies or technical logs under their respective policies.

10. User-entered content

You control the information added to repertoires, notes and lead sheets. Do not enter third-party personal information, sensitive data, or material you are not authorized to use. Synchronization copies the content saved to your MIDI HUD library into the selected Google Drive account.

11. Changes and contact

This policy applies to the current web beta described above and may change as features or providers change. The update date will be revised, and material changes will be communicated within the application or on its public website where appropriate. Privacy contact: midihudapp@gmail.com.

Relevant law: RGPD/GDPR · Lei portuguesa n.º 58/2019.
